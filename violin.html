The violin has four strings: G, D, A and E, from lowest to highest. Pressing a finger on the fingerboard makes the string shorter, so the note goes higher. In Bow mode the note keeps sounding while you hold, just like drawing the bow across the string. In Pluck mode you pick the string with your finger.

On the violin picture, tap the strings between the end of the fingerboard and the bridge to play them open, or tap a gold dot to play that finger. On a computer, use the keyboard: 1 Q W E R for the G string, 2 A S D F for the D string, 3 Z X C V for the A string and 4 U I O P for the E string (open, then fingers 1 to 4).
