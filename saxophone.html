On a saxophone, closing more keys makes the tube longer and the note lower. The octave key (thumb) plays the same fingering one octave higher. The alto sax is an E♭ instrument: a written C sounds as the concert E♭ a major sixth lower. E♭ and G♯ need pinky or side keys that aren’t shown here, so they’re not in the note buttons yet.
