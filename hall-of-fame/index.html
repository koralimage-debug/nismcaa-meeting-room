7th Edition

Imperial Lights College

Imperial Lights College ended the streak and lifted the 7th Edition NISMCAA trophy! 🎶

Founded in April 2017 by a group of professionals and veteran educationists, including Assistant Pastor Olayemi Abiola Ashaolu, this co-educational day secondary school is guided by an experienced management team. Its purpose-built campus has modern science and ICT labs, a library, a sick bay, a multipurpose hall and an outdoor sports complex. Students learn from local and British curricula, with Cambridge-certified teachers for core classes, and prepare for WASSCE, NECO, IGCSE, Checkpoint, SAT and TOEFL.

Music training shines alongside JETS, chess, Scrabble, ballet and excursions! 🎵 Parents rate it 4.6 stars for affordable tuition, well-organized sports and a supportive, disciplined home that nurtures young leaders.
