Inglewood Academy, Ikorodu

Inglewood Academy, Ikorodu, is the secondary school of the Inglewood Group of Schools. It was founded in September 2002, and the group teaches students ages 2 to 18 across its campuses.

In the 2nd Edition of NISMCAA, Inglewood Academy took the title and proved that great music can come from every corner of the city. 🎶
