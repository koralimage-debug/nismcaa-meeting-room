9th Edition · Joint Champion

Honeyland College

Champion of the 4th, 5th and 6th Editions, and joint champion of the 9th Edition.

Honeyland College shared the 9th Edition title with Lagoon School, the first time in NISMCAA history that two schools were crowned joint champions. 🎶

Honeyland has served families since 2006 across several campuses, and music is a big part of its story: the school lists music awards among its proudest achievements. The 9th Edition joint title adds to its 4th, 5th and 6th Edition wins.
