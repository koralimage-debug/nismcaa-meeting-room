Bass Guitar

Bass Guitar
📱 Turn your phone sideways

The bass guitar has four strings: E, A, D and G, from lowest to highest, one octave lower than a guitar's lowest four. It is about 46 inches long. Each fret raises the note by a half step.

How to play on the screen: press a fret on the neck with one finger and pluck that string on the body with another, just like a real bass. Swipe up or down across the strings to strum. Use several fingers at once. Turn your phone sideways for the biggest bass. On a computer: 1 Q W E R for the E string, 2 A S D F for A, 3 Z X C V for D, 4 U I O P for G.
