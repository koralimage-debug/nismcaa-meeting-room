The bass guitar has four strings: E, A, D and G, from lowest to highest. They are tuned like the lowest four strings of a guitar, but one octave lower. Each fret raises the note by a half step. In first position, finger 1 plays fret 1, finger 2 plays fret 2, and so on: one finger per fret.

On the bass picture, tap a string between the pickups to play it open, or tap a gold dot on the neck to play that fret. On a computer, use the keyboard: 1 Q W E R for the E string, 2 A S D F for the A string, 3 Z X C V for the D string and 4 U I O P for the G string (open, then frets 1 to 4).
