🎺 Meet the French horn

  • 🐘 If you unrolled all its tubing, a French horn would be about 12 feet long, taller than a giraffe!
  • 🖐️ It's the only brass instrument you play with your left hand on the valves and your right hand inside the bell.
  • 🔄 The bell points backward, so the sound bounces off the wall behind you and comes out warm and round.
  • 🎬 You hear French horns in movie music like Star Wars and Jurassic Park.

How the valves work: each valve opens extra tubing to make the note lower. Valve 2 lowers it a half step, valve 1 a whole step, and valve 3 one and a half steps. Your air picks which family of notes you get.

How to play on the screen: hold an air pad or the bell with one hand and press valves 1, 2 and 3 with the other. Turn your phone sideways for the biggest horn. On a computer: hold the space bar to blow, hold keys 1, 2, 3 (or J, K, L) for the valves, and use the up and down arrows to change the air. Notes are named the way horn players read them (horn in F); the real sound is five notes lower.
