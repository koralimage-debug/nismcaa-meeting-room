Trombone

Trombone
📱 Turn your phone sideways

A trombone has no keys or valves. You change the note by moving the slide and by how you buzz your lips. The slide has 7 positions: 1 is all the way in, and each position out is a half step lower. A trombone has about 9 feet of tubing folded into about 3½ feet.

How to play on the screen: hold a buzz pad or the bell to blow, and drag the slide with another finger. Glide the slide while blowing for the famous trombone slide sound. Turn your phone sideways for the biggest trombone. On a computer: hold the space bar to blow, keys 1 to 7 move the slide, and the up and down arrows change the buzz.
