A trombone has no keys or valves. You change the note two ways: by moving the slide, and by how you buzz your lips. The slide has 7 positions. Position 1 is all the way in, and each position farther out makes the note a half step lower. Buzzing your lips tighter and faster makes a higher note in the same position.

On the trombone picture, tap a slide position number from 1 to 7 to play it with the buzz you picked (low, middle or high). On a computer, use the keyboard: keys 1 to 7 for low buzz, Q W E R T Y U for middle buzz and A S D F G H J for high buzz (positions 1 to 7).
