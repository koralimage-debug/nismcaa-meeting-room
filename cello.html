The cello has four strings: C, G, D and A, from lowest to highest. On the photo, the C string is on the right, because the cello faces you. Pressing a finger on the fingerboard makes the string shorter, so the note goes higher. In first position, finger 1 is a whole step above the open string, and fingers 2, 3 and 4 each go up a half step.

On the cello photo, tap a string between the end of the fingerboard and the bridge to play it open, or tap a gold dot on the fingerboard to play that finger. On a computer, use the keyboard: 1 Q W E R for the C string, 2 A S D F for the G string, 3 Z X C V for the D string and 4 U I O P for the A string (open, then fingers 1 to 4).
