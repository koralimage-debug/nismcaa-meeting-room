NISMCAA Music Competition

School of the Decade

Every school that has won the NISMCAA Music Competition is in the running. Vote for your favorite. The school with the most votes is crowned NISMCAA School of the Decade.

Award rules

  • Candidates are the winning schools of the 1st to 9th editions of the NISMCAA Music Competition. Schools that won more than once appear once.
  • One vote per person. Duplicate or automated votes are removed.
  • Voting closes at the deadline shown above. The school with the most valid votes wins the NISMCAA School of the Decade award.
  • Voting is free. NISMCAA's decision on vote validity is final.
