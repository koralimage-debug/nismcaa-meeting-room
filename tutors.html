For tutors

1. Profile & certify

Make your profile, pass the subject test and send a short teaching demo. Only NISMCAA Certified tutors are listed.

2. Set your rate

Your price per hour, in your own currency.

3. Teach

Learners book your open slots.

4. Get paid

NISMCAA keeps 20% of your price and you keep 80%. Tax is added on top and paid by the learner.

Commission: % of each booking. of the tutor's lesson price. Tutors keep %. Tax is added on top at checkout, paid by the learner and set aside separately (e.g. $50 lesson: learner pays $50 + tax; NISMCAA $10, tutor $40).
